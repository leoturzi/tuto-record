tuto-record

Saves a screenshot of every display each time you click.

Permissions

tuto-record needs both permissions below. macOS asked for them on launch.

Accessibility

Lets tuto-record notice your clicks in any app.

Screen Recording

Lets tuto-record take the screenshots.

Output folder

Screenshots are saved directly in this folder.

Recording

Each left click saves one screenshot per display. The window hides while recording. Stop with ⌃⌥⌘R or by clicking the Dock icon.
